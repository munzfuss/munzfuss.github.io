# Danish denomination grids 1403–1700 — common and separate grids, transitions, the ratios behind them

> **Status:** research dossier (role-1), opened 2026-11-12 at the curator's request: «вичленувати в
> періоді (не обмежуйсь 1541 роком, йди далі аж до кінця 17 століття) спільні та окремі сітки, більш
> повно з урахуванням всіх номіналів в періодах, роки переходів, обрахунки співвідношень».
> **Scope:** which Danish (and Norwegian) denominations formed ONE grid and which stood in SEPARATE
> grids, period by period from 1403 to 1700; the year of each transition; the set ratios and the
> silver computations each conclusion rests on. The Danish : Lübeck tie is treated as a grid question.
> **Excluded:** the full ratio chains per act to 1625 — `denomination_ratio_chains_1403_1625.md` (this
> dossier reads them and extends to 1700); the formula series of each line —
> `danish_small_coin_lines_1497_1660.md`; act-by-act parameters — `instruments/<yyyy>_*.md` (every grid
> figure below is taken from the §4 of the instrument dossier named, recomputed there); gold standards
> (only where gold is tariffed against a grid); the 18th century (the 1726 Kurantdaler and the 1737
> Krone agio are named only as the end of the last split).
> «[C]» = computed here. Cologne mark 233,855 g. Fine silver per Skilling (g/ß) is the comparison unit
> for the metal test: the coin's fine weight divided by its value in ß as SET.

## 1. Criterion

- **One grid:** denominations linked to one base by **integer ratios set by an act** (or by the coin's
  own name where the act names the sort by value). The SET / GRID / obs. labels of
  `denomination_ratio_chains_1403_1625.md` apply.
- **Separate grids:** two sets of denominations each integer inside itself, joined to each other only by
  a ratio that is (a) not integer in the units of either, or (b) changes from act to act while each set
  stays fixed inside, or (c) is a market rate / agio with no act.
- **Metal test (supporting, never decisive):** inside one grid, g/ß falls smoothly from large to small
  coin (Scheidemünze gradient). A step that coincides with a floating tariff link marks the seam.
- The criterion is the curator's (2026-11-05: Fuß = formula + inertial continuity; units not sub-units
  of each other = two grids). Applying it to the material below is this dossier's work; each «grid»
  verdict is a reading of SET ratios, not a term found in the sources.

## 4. c. 1481/1497 – 1536: two grids

**Grid A (Hvid):** Hvid; Blaffert = ½ Hvid (Galster's reading for 1514; SET only 1602); Mark dansk =
16 ß à 3 Hvide = 48 Hvid (Wilcke 7-2 s. 149, obs. under Hans; Oslo 1525 SET: G = 120 Hvid = 2½ M);
Mark pieces 1524 (9,301 g = 47,7 Hvid [C]) and 2-Mark pieces; Christian II's and Christian III's Mark
Klippinge 1518–36.
**Grid B (ß, Lübeck count):** Pf; Søsling = ½ ß; ß; 2, 4, 8 ß; Sølvgylden = G = 24 ß (SET 1514; G =
24 ß l. obs. Ribe 1501; SET 1523).

| Seam: ß : Hvid | Year | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 4 («*4 Hvide ß, som Kong Hans mønte lod*») then 5 | 1497 → c. 1500 | obs. |
| 3 | 1513 (Blicher), 1514 (grid 0,996 / 0,332) | SET; GRID |
| 6 old Hvid per new ß | 1515 | SET |
| 4 | 1523 (Valvation); 1524 «fyre huider skellinge» 3,99 [GRID] | SET; GRID |
| G : M = 2 → 2½ | 1513 SET 2; Hans obs. 2½; Oslo 1525 SET 2½; accounts 1534–36 2½ | SET / obs. |

Metal [C]:
- Hans 1512: ß 0,91–1,04 g against Hvid 0,206 g → 4,4–5,0 Hvid per ß, the obs. «5 Hvide».
- Søsling 1506 0,507 g against ß 1512 ≈ 0,97 g → ½ ß. Søsling against Hvid → 2,5 Hvid, never 1½.
  The Søsling belongs to grid B.
- 1514: ß 0,997 g = 3,00 Hvid (0,332); Sølvgylden 24,07 g = 24,1 ß = 72,5 Hvid: the two grids meet on
  one silver level, the only such moment.
- 1524: Mark piece 9,301 g = 11,95 four-Hvid ß; Sølvgylden 25,58 g = 32,9 four-Hvid ß against the
  Oslo tariff 30, i.e. 2,75 M by silver against 2½ M set.

1523 sets one full chain (Pf 3 – Hvid 4 – ß 12 – M 2 – G) and so tries to put both grids on one base;
it has Pf d. = 1⅓ Pf l. and Mark = 12 ß, is called by Wilcke «*en ejendommelig Blanding af dansk og
lybsk „Wehrung“*» («a peculiar mixture of Danish and Lübeck currency»), and was not executed.
1524–25 defines the Skilling coin in Hvide (four-Hvid ß): the ß coin moves into grid A, the Gylden stays
loose (30 such ß = 2½ M).
**Placement of the early-grid denominations under the split:** Søsling → B; sterling (3 Pf, ¼ ß) → B if
assigned at all (¾ Hvid non-integer); Penning → B (12 per ß; 3 or 4 per Hvid floats); gros → none (a
tariffed episode); Skærv → no tariff known.

## 6. 1602 – Nov 1619: two grids, the Rdl against the ß

**Grid C (Rdl):** Rdl in specie; ½; Ort (¼); **Mark piece = ¼ Rdl** («*fire mark danske lige saa møgit
som en rigsdaller*», Secher III nr. 208, 1605; ½ M = ⅛ Rdl; 4 M = 1 Rdl again 1616); from 1618 the
Krone = 1½ Rdl «*al tid*», ½ Krone ¾, Orts Krone ⅜, ⅛ and 1/16 Krone.
**Grid D (ß):** Blaffert = 2 Pf, Hvid = 4 Pf = ⅓ ß, Søsling = 6 Pf = ½ ß, ß = 12 Pf, 2, 4, 8 ß; the
Mark of account = 16 ß (SET, the 25 June 1602 schedule computes in Marks of 16 ß).

- 8 Sept 1602 (Rdl 25,984 g at 66 ß): Rdl 0,394; fine Mark (target ¼ Rdl 6,496 g) 0,394 / struck

  Mark was meant to be exactly ¼ Rdl in silver («*skulde være nøje ¼ Daler og indeholde 6,496 gr. fint

- 1618 (Rdl 84 ß): Rdl 0,309; Krone 32,500 g / 126 = 0,258; Mark piece 5,143 / 20 = 0,257; 4 ß 0,241;
  2 ß 0,219; ß 0,186; Søsling 0,170; Hvid 0,159. Wilcke: «*At Kronerne var akkurat saa slette som
  Markerne*» — Krone and Mark piece on one level, both 17 % under the Rdl.

**Apr – 15 Nov 1619: a third grid.** Krone = 12 half-Ort = 24 four-ß = 48 two-ß = 96 Kron-ß (Frdg.
8 Apr 1619), copper Hvid ⅓ Kron-ß, Blaffert ½ Hvid; Rdl = 64 Kron-ß. Beside it the daler-ß (Rdl = 84)
with the billon Hvid at 4 per ß. Kron-ß : daler-ß = 1 5/16 [computed, W I s. 166] — two Skillinge, two
Hvide (⅓ of one, ¼ of the other). Separate grids by criterion (a).

**Nov 1619 merge.** Rdl = 96 ß = 6 M, Krone 144 ß = 1½ Rdl = 9 M, Sletdaler 64 ß = 4 M (Frdg. 16 Nov
1619; 1621; 1622). Grid C and D now share the Skilling: one grid. The pre-1619 pieces become off-grid
tariffed coins: Mark piece 20 → 21 ß; 4 ß and 2 ß «of payement» 3½ and 1¾ ß.
1624: «hver daler til 100 sk.» with the Krone kept at 144 (Wilcke's inference) — the Rdl steps off the
grid by a 4-ß opgæld. 4 May 1625: Rdl 96 «*oc icke mere*», Krone 2 Sletdaler = 128 ß — the Krone is
now pegged to the Mark (8 M), no longer to the Rdl (1⅓, not 1½).

## 8. 1647 – 1700: specie and kurant

**The first set seam.** The Hebræer coin of 1644 (2 M 21/M, 1 M 42/M at 9½ lod; 4 ß 120/M at 4 lod)
carries face values ⅓, ⅙, 1/24 Rdl, with g/ß 0,207 (2 M) and 0,122 (4 ß) against the specie's 0,265
[C]. Frdg. 10 Dec 1647 sets the Rdl at «*6 mrk. ½ udi mynt*» = **104 ß** while the gold stays rated in
specie Rdl — two dalers in one act; 2 M = 32/104 = 4/13 Rdl (non-integer). Frdg. 21 March 1648 returns
the Rdl to 96 and moves the coins: 2 M → 28 ß, 1 M → 14 ß, 4 ß → 2 ß (g/ß 0,236 / 0,236 / 0,244 [C]);
the coin names no longer carry their value in the grid.

**Grid F (kurant) from 1651–52:** Krone = Sletdaler = 4 M = ⅔ Rdl (10½/M at 10¾ lod, 1652), 2 M
(⅓ Rdl), 1 M (1675, 42/M), 8 ß (84/M on the Krone grid, Christiania 1685), 2 ß (192/M at 4½ lod,
1648/53), 1 ß (256/M at 2½ lod), copper Søsling and Hvid (1651); after 1671: 4 ß, 2 ß, 1 ß, Søsling on
the Myndt-Ordning grid; 1686 2 ß, 1 ß, ½ ß, Hvid; 1692 fine Kroner and Mark pieces; 1694/95 8 ß, 4 ß, 24 ß
(the 24 ß first struck 1731). All integer in ß and in Marks: one grid nominally.
**Grid E (specie):** Speciedaler (8/M at 14 lod to 1670; 8 3/32 at 14 lod, 1671), ½, ¼, ⅛; the 1648
Rigsort (24 ß) «*af fint Rigsdalersølv*» — on E in 1648, the 24 ß of the 1695 grid on F.
