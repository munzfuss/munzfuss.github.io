**Important scope correction.** (1) The 1764 assays in (B) are **NOT Erik VII coins** — they are eight

   s. 627–633, 636–662, 666–678, 680–697 (incl. s. 683 deed «pro quolibet grosso tres sterlingos, & pro quolibet
   sterlingo tres denarios Lubicenses» — OCR only, not checked; and s. 653 «Fra 1403 til 1411»). The 1390 table
   (s. 621) and any further Erik VII material there are not transcribed.
2. Fractions illegible at 150–300 dpi: Witten fine Es and value (s. 700 note 1); 1403 gold-table header (s. 623);
   some ratio fractions in the 1453 table (s. 710). Would be settled by a 600-dpi crop of each.
3. Gold off-strike: Luxdorph 23⅔ vs Lindahl's «23½ til 23¾» citing him — whether Lindahl misquoted or used
   another Luxdorph text; and «228½ Eß» vs computed 178½.

   the computation from his own Rpf; and No. 5 fineness glyph (3 8/9 by the per-fine-mark count). Settled by a
   600-dpi crop.
5. «Recessus Hansæ» = a manuscript in Count Holstein-Ledreborg's library (s. 616), fols 365a (1403) and 368 (gold);
   its relation to HR I 5 Nr. 115 / Lüb. UB 5 Nr. 66 (both from other originals) not established.

   Praun 1739 p. 118 and Leibniz *Script. Brunsv.* III would settle it.
