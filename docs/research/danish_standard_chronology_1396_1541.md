# The Danish coin standard under the Lübeck orientation, 1396–1541 — chronology of the formulas

> **Status:** internal research dossier (role-1), opened 2026-10-28 at the curator's request: the Danish
> counterpart of `luebeck_standard_chronology_1226_1622.md` — every act, rate and assay that fixes the Danish
> silver from the restart of the royal coinage (c. 1396/1400) to the point where Denmark stopped orienting its
> coin on the Lübeck one. It is a COMPILATION: most rows rest on dossiers already written (pointers in the
> «where» column, verbatim quotes there); what this file adds is the single series, the Lübeck level beside
> each Danish level [C], the matrix of denominations, and the end-point question (§5).

## §0. Scope

**Settles:** for Denmark (and Norway where an act binds it) 1396–1541 — instrument or evidence, coin formula
(pieces per weighed mark / fineness), fine weight, the level in marks of 16 ß per fine Cologne mark [C], the
Gylden rate, and the Lübeck level of the same date, so that «following / below / above Lübeck» is visible per
row.

**Excluded, with their home:** gold (`nobel_fod.md`, `rhinsk_gylden_fod.md`, `danish_royal_gold_1560_1648.md`);
the Swedish ørtug of the union kings (`instruments/1480_moentordning_arboga.md`, `…/1497_moentanordning_stockholm.md`);
the ducal coin of Schleswig-Holstein (Husum 1514–22, Gottorp, Flensborg 1545–71 — `sh_ducal_zone_husum_1514.md`,
`denmark_fuesse_year_boundaries.md` §2) except where a royal act names it; Gotland (`gotland_gote.md`); the
detail of King Hans' assays and rates (`luebischer_muenzfuss.md` §0.6–§0.7, `hans_rates_1481_1513.md`); the
war coin of 1534–39 beyond its table (`instruments/1537_reces_moentens_afsaettelse.md`); 1541 onwards
(`moentordning_1541.md`, `christian_iii_danish_coinage_1534_1572.md`) — only the rate tie to the Lübeck ß is
followed past 1541, in §5, for the end-point question.

## §3. What the series shows [C, «можна вважати»]

1. **Three kinds of tie, in sequence.** (a) 1424: a common formula — Danish coin = Lübeck coin by treaty (the only
   such case); (b) 1460–1523: a par clause without parameters — Môtbok art. 81 / § 67 (1460–74, 1484), the
   Holstein privilege 1460 (duchies), Frederik I's håndfæstning 1523 «*lige god ved lybsk*»; (c) the reckoning —
   Gylden in Lübeck ß (23 ß 1474, 24 Skilling coins 1483–1513, 24 ß 1514, 24 new ß 1523). 1536 replaces Lübeck in
   the clause by «*Fyrstendømmernes Mønt*»; 1541 states marks and skillings «*i dansk mønt, ikke lybsk*».
2. **Levels: the Danish Skilling meets Lübeck twice, the Hvid never after Christopher.** Skilling 1497 at 11,5–11,8
   (Lübeck 11,5); 1514 grid 14,67 against Lübeck 13–13,25; 1524 Sølvgylden 13,71 (better than Lübeck 15,9 of 1525),
   but the 4-Hvid ß of the same letter 18,8 (W: «*til Dels Kreditmønt*»). The Hvid runs 17 → 20–24 → 16–18 → 20–30
   from Christopher to Hans, below every Lübeck level of its time.
3. **1513 is a break in reckoning, not in the tie.** Blicher's grid (32 ß per Gylden, W «*Forholdet til den lybske ß
   ophævet*») was not carried out; 1514 returns to 24 ß = 72 Hvide per Gylden. 1523–24 is the second break
   (Mark = 12 ß) — with the explicit Lübeck clause in the same year.
4. **1541 is the first act whose unit is not the Lübeck one.** Daler = 3 M d = 48 ß d; the Danish ß reckoned at
   ½ ß lübisch (M s. 488). By silver [C]: the Lübeck Schilling of 1546 stands at 19,6 Lübeck marks per fine mark,
   which at 2 ß d per ß l. is 39,3 Danish marks; the Danish Mark of 1541 stands at 26,5 — Danish coin better than
   the 2 : 1 implies. The 2 : 1 is a reckoning rate, not a silver equality.
5. **Before 1424 the Danish coin sat about 1½ × below Lübeck**: sterling 6,1–9,8 against 5,4 (1406) [C], matching
   the 1406 tariff of the sterling at 2 instead of 3 Pf l.; the 1406 editor's 16,2 does not fit this and is
   flagged in §4.

## §5. The end of the Lübeck orientation — candidates (for the curator)

What each candidate rests on:

| Year | Event | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1513 | Christian II: 32 ß per Gylden, «*Forholdet til den lybske ß ophævet*» (W) | first act detaching the Danish ß | not carried out; 1514 returns to 24 ß per Gylden |
| 1523–24 | Mark = 12 ß; «*Blanding af dansk og lybsk Wehrung*» | the Mark of account changes | the same year's håndfæstning ties the coin «*lige god ved lybsk*» |
| 1536 | håndfæstning names «*Fyrstendømmernes Mønt*», not Lübeck | the last tie clause, and it is to the duchies | the duchies themselves were bound to Lübeck/Hamburg (1460 art. 9; Flensborg 1547 «*als Hamburg, Lübeck und Lüneburg*») — an indirect tie |
| **1541** | Møntordning: Daler at the head, 3 M d = 48 ß d, units «*i dansk mønt, ikke lybsk*» (M s. 488); Galster: unity «*with the duchies, where efforts had continuously been made to maintain parity with the Lübeck coin, and with all of Germany, where the daler had completely triumphed*» | first Danish unit system not defined by the Lübeck mark; head coin is the daler | the Danish ß keeps a fixed rate to the Lübeck ß (2 : 1) |
| after 1541 | the 2 : 1 rate is restated in acts: 12 May 1602 every daler = 33 ß lybsk = 66 sk danske; 3 Feb 1609 daler = 34 ß lybsk; Glückstadt 1619 Denning = 1 ß l. = 2 ß d.; 1625 Rigsdaler = 6 M d (96 ß d) against 48 ß l. | Danish small coin still reckoned in Lübeck ß | the rate is a tariff between two systems, not a Danish standard set on Lübeck's; the Danish grid is set in its own unit (1544, 1582, 1602 ff.) and from 1618 on the Krone |

**Working end of this dossier: 1541** — chosen on M's own statement and the change of unit; the evidence for a
later end (the 2 : 1 rate in acts to 1625 and beyond) is listed above and in `ordinances_register.md` §1b. The
curator decides; if a later end is chosen, the rows to add are the §1b register rows of 1544–1625 that state a
rate in ß lybsk.
