# The Lübeck (Wendish) coin standard 1226–1622 — chronology of the coin formulas

> **Status:** internal research dossier (role-1), opened 2026-10-28 at the curator's request: every recess,
> treaty, article and ordinance that dictates a coin formula («N pieces from the X-lot mark») in the Lübeck
> system from c. 1350 to c. 1600, because Denmark built its silver on it (`luebischer_muenzfuss.md`;
> `wendischer_muenzverein_c1400.md` holds the c. 1400 detail and is superseded by this file for the series).

## §0. Scope

**Settles:** the chronology of the Lübeck / Wendish silver standard — instrument, members, coin formula,
fine weight, fine mark in ℳ lüb. — from the earliest dated value (1226) to the Lower Saxon circle rules of
1617/1622, with the c. 1350–1600 span complete as far as the sources go.

**Excluded, with their home:** Danish coins (`luebischer_muenzfuss.md`); gold except where a recess ties the
Gulden to the Mark; the sundish (Rostock / Stralsund) system beyond its assays; 1622–1726 (Bobzin page, not
researched here).

## §2. The chronology (revised 2026-10-28 with Jesse 1928)

**Jesse's tables are the backbone** (`jesse-1928`, Tabellen S. 209–213: I Pfennig, II Witten, III Sechslinge /
Dreilinge, IV Schillinge / Doppelschillinge, V Markgepräge, VI Silberpreis / Kosten / Gewinn with the source of
each line). Columns: formula = pieces per weighed mark / fineness in lot; «J» = Jesse's fine mark (Mark fein zu);
[C] = recomputed here on 233,856 g; Gr = Grautoff's table (S. 265–266, * = gold-derived). Status: **R** recess /
treaty text read (HR); **J** Jesse table or text; **Gd** Gaedechens; **Gr** Grautoff; «plan» = drafted, not
carried out (Jesse). A row marked «plan» did not govern any coinage.

## §3. What the series shows [C, «можна вважати»]

Fine mark in ℳ lüb.: 2,1 (1226) → 3,0 (1324) → 3,6–3,8 (1346–1364) → 4,2–4,5 (1372–1387; Witten 176 / 13–13½) → 4,75 (1392) → 4,95
(1398) → 5,1–5,8 (1403–1411) → 7–8 (1418–1424) → 8,5 (1430) → 9,2–9,8 (1432–1450) → 10 (c. 1450/51; 1463 plan) →
11,5 (**1468**, 1492, 1502; 1461 plan) → **13** (1504/06, 1512) → 13¼ (1515) → 15–16 (1520s–1550s) → 18 (1572,
Taler parity, 1 Taler = 32 ß) → 21,5–27 (1610–1622). The fineness of the main coin falls in steps: Witten 13-lot
(1398) → 12½ (1403) → 12 (1406–1411); Sechsling 12 / 11¼ (1423/24); Schilling 10-lot (1432–1451), 15-lot (1450,
1463), 9-lot (1461–1492), 8-lot (1515, 1572), 7-lot (1551).

**For Denmark:** the coin copied c. 1400 is the Witten of the **1398** Satzung (13-lot, 193 per mark, 0,985 g
fine), between 1392 (penny 0,254 g) and 1403 (0,952 g). Hans' Skilling of c. 1497 equals the 1468/1492 Schilling
(103½ / 9-lot, 1,271 g); the 13-Mark step is the 1504/1506 Markpfennig.
