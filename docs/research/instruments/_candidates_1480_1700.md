# Candidate instruments 1480–1700 — triage for the register (Stage 2, 2026-10-13)

Working list, not the register. Every dated instrument found in the cached
sources (Wilcke VII / I / II, Galster, Ernst/Holst articles, Secher CCD
Sagregister «Møntvæsenet» Bd 1–6, Mordhorst 1661–1699), triaged against the
scope rule (sets pieces/mark, fineness, weight, tariff/course, legal tender
or demonetisation, a mint, a mintmaster WITH parameters):

- **✅ in scope** — becomes a register row.
- **❓ check** — in scope only if the text sets a parameter; read before deciding.
- **✖ out** — goes to the register's §4 «seen, excluded» with the reason.

Jurisdiction codes as in the register: DK · NO · SH-d · SH-k · HRE · SE (Sweden,
Kalmar-Union years, evidentiary only). «Text» = where the ACT can be read:
`Secher N p.` is Secher's printed page (from the Sagregister; PDF offset
per volume still to be fixed), `W7-2 p.N` = Wilcke VII chapter 2, PDF page N
of our extract, `W1 p.N` = Wilcke I PDF page, `W2 w2a1` = Wilcke II chapter
file, `Mordh.` = Mordhorst digest.
