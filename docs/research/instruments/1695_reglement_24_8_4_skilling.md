# The Reglement of 4 May 1695 for the 24, 8 and 4 Skilling — the 11⅓-Rigsdaler-kurant grid read off the coins (text not in hand)

> The **Reglement of 4 May 1695** is known from one clause of Schou's 1795 footnote: «*24 Skill.,
> 8 Skill. og 4 Skill. Stkr efter Regl. 4 Maj. 1695*» — the rule under which the 24, 8 and 4 ß in
> circulation in 1795 had been struck. It is in no register of printed acts (not in Schou's 1695
> chronology, not in Mordhorst) and Hede 1957 does not cite it. What it set has to be read off the
> coins, and the coins give one grid: **8 ß at 76½ to the 9-lot mark (3,057 g · 0,5625 · 1,7196 g
> fine) — 136 pieces = 1 088 ß = 11⅓ Rigsdaler per mark fine** (Hede's «*Marken fin udbragt til
> 11,333 rigsdaler kurant*»), struck at København 1695 (Hede 113, «Udmøntet 50.000 rd.»), at
> Glückstadt 1694–97 (Hede 127, «Udmøntet ialt 760.000 rd.») and, unchanged, under Frederik IV
> 1700–13 at København, Glückstadt and Kongsberg (Hede 42, 58, Norge 6A); the **24 ß** at 25½ to the
> 9-lot mark (9,171 g · 5,159 g fine) first struck 1731 (Christian VI, Hede 6–7) and the **4 ß** at
> 85 to the 5-lot mark (2,751 g · 0,860 g fine) first struck 1727–30 (Hede 52), both at the same
> 11⅓ — the København 4 ß of 1696 (Hede 114) is a Prøvemønt. So the 11⅓-Rdl.-kurant rate that our
> `11_333_thaler` card dates «1726 → 1813» is on the 8 ß from 1694/95 — the Reglement's grid is
> the Kurantmøntfod's, thirty-one years before the Forordning of 15/31 July 1726 (whose own
> content is §1d's dossier, not this one). That the grid IS the Reglement's is a hypothesis of the
> same kind as in `1673_…` (§4.1). Register row: §1, 1695 (4 May) added. **Scope:** the footnote
> attestation, the grid read off Hede 113 / 127 / 42 / 58 / Norge 6 / 52 / C6 6–7 with recomputation,
> the Glückstadt precedent of 1693–94, our rows 1693–1743 under the three nominals. **Excluded:**
> the Reskript 31 Dec 1692 (`1692_…`), the Forordning 15/31 July 1726 (§1d, to write), the
> Kongsberg 8 ß of 1727–35 at 0,468 (Hede Norge 2 / 16–17 — a different grid, for the 1726
> dossier), the 12 ß of 1710–19 (Hede 43; reduced 1726). Written 2026-11-04 from Schou I s. 80 n.
> (*) and s. 665–667 (IIIF 0715–0717: no 4 May entry), Mordhorst c5 (1693–99: no Reglement), Hede
> c5h113, 114, 127, 128, f4h42, 52, 58, Norge f4h6, c6h6, c6h7, Galster's type pages (24skill,
> 8skill, 4skill), Galster 1973 s. 48.

## 4. Parameters

### 4.1 The grid read off the coins (TARGET by hypothesis; Hede's figures recompute exactly)

Recomputed at 233,855 g; 1 Rdl. = 96 ß; «11,333 Rdl. kurant per mark fine» = 1 088 ß per mark fine.

Every printed figure recomputes from the count: 3,057 = 233,855 / 76,5; 9,171 = / 25,5; 2,751 =
/ 85; 2,292 = / 102 — grid values, not weighings; and the three nominals struck 1695–1743 share
one line, 1 088 ß = 11⅓ Rdl. per mark fine, with the 8 ß and 24 ß at 9 lod and the 4 ß at 5 lod.
**Hypothesis:** this is R's grid. What supports it: (a) Schou names R as the rule for exactly
these three sorts, still in force in 1795; (b) the København 8 ß of 1695 (R's year) and every
8 ß to 1713, the 4 ß of 1727–30 and the 24 ß from 1731 all sit on it; (c) no other act for the
8 / 24 / 4 ß between 1692 and 1726 is named in Schou, Mordhorst or Hede. What would settle it:
R's text. What weakens it: the Glückstadt 8 ß is dated 1694 (Hede 127A/B) — the grid was in use
at Glückstadt before 4 May 1695, as the 1686 grid was at Christiania in 1684 (`1684_…` D4) and
R's 1673 grid at Christiania in 1673 (`1673_…` D6): in each case the København Reglement names a
rate a provincial mint already struck.

The 11⅓ line is the «Rigsdaler kurant» of Hede's wording: 1 Rdl. kurant = 96 ß at 233,855 / 11⅓ =
**20,634 g fine** — the figure of our `11_333_thaler` card (20,63435 g). The card dates the
standard «1726 → 1813» and credits it to the Forordning of 15/31 July 1726; **the 8 ß carries the
rate from 1694/95 and the 24 ß of 1731 is struck on R's grid, not on a new one** — what the 1726
Forordning added (the 12 ß and 2 ß reduced, the Kurantdaler as unit) is for its own dossier (§1d);
the Kurantmøntfod's grid for the 8 / 4 / 24 ß is R's (⚠ D4).

## 5. Mints and mintmasters

København 1695: mintmaster not named on Hede 113 (the dies of the 1693 coin are Meybusch's —
Hede 108–110; Märcher 2012 on the 1690s); Christian Wineke (CW) on the 1727–30 4 ß (Hede 52) and
the 1731 24 ß (Hede 6A: Wineke's heart on the shield, which Christian VI «misbilligede» — Hede
6B without it); the dies of 1731–43 by J. H. Wahl (Hede 6, 7). Glückstadt 1694–97: «korslagte
glødehager» (Hede 127). Kongsberg 1700–15: Hede Norge 6.

## 8. Open questions

1. **R's text** — a Reglement to the København mint, 4 May 1695; archive signature unknown;
   would settle the counts (76½ / 25½ / 85) and whether R named the 24 ß and 4 ß that were not
   struck for circulation until 1731 / 1727 (D7).
2. **The Glückstadt 8 ß of 1694** — on R's grid a year before R; the Glückstadt order (Mordhorst's
   list is Danish only) is the missing act; the 760 000 rd. figure's source (D6).
3. **The relation to the Forordning of 15/31 July 1726** — which of the Kurantmøntfod's elements
   are R's (the 11⅓ rate for the 8 / 4 / 24 ß) and which 1726's (the unit, the reductions, the
   12 ß) — the §1d dossier; the `11_333_thaler` card's start date waits on it.

   withdrawn or ran beside R's is not in the sources read.
