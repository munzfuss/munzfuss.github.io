# Vertrag of 28 March 1535 (Schwerin) — Duke Albrecht VII of Mecklenburg and the Güstrow mintmaster Moritz Schacht: «dänische Münz» in Christian II's name

> The only parameter-bearing coinage instrument of Christian II's side in the Count's Feud
> (Grevens Fejde) that survives: a contract of Albrecht VII of Mecklenburg with his Güstrow
> mintmaster Moritz Schacht to strike, «*inn nahmen unsers hern, Khenig Christierns seiner
> unschuldigen gefengknus*», four-skilling pieces of Danish currency, ten to the Gulden, at
> 63½ to the mark and 4½ Lot fine. Struck March–August 1535 (117 610 pieces by Kunzel's count)
> and shipped to Denmark. Kunzel 1985–86 identifies the coin as **Galster 85** (4 Skilling
> 1535, Güstrow). Register row: §1a, 1535 (28 March).
> **Scope:** the contract, its parameters recomputed, the production accounts as Kunzel
> reports them, the rates and the 1536 declaration that bear on the coin, the comparison with
> Danish, Mecklenburg and Lübeck standards of the same years, the other Count's-Feud coins
> whose weights fit the contract figure, our data. **Excluded:** the Copenhagen
> «Beutepfennige» (Galster 84 — only their identification is mentioned, §6), Christian III's
> war coin as such (Junge's accounts: `1537_reces_moentens_afsaettelse.md`, Galster 1934), the
> Kopenhagen lead bulla of 1535 (Kunzel's last section).
> Written 2026-11-06 from Kunzel 1985–86 (read in danskmoent's reproduction, cached), Jesse
> 1928, Wilcke VII (1950) kap. 3, Galster 1934 (Junge), *Fra Denar til Daler* (2018). The
> archive (StA Schwerin, Acta monetaria Vol. XVI) not seen.

## 3. Verbatim text

**V — operative clause** (Kunzel, gustrow.htm, section «a) Die Prägung dänischer Münzen in
Mecklenburg»): «*(...) wir (...) mit unserm Munzmeister zu Gustrow und lieben getrewen,
Mauritz Schachten der dänischen Münz halben, uns zumunzen, inn nahmen unsers hern, Khenig
Christierns seiner unschuldigen gefengknus, Nachvolgender weis und gestalt, ubereinkhomen
sein, das er uns soll gelt machen, der da zehen stuck ein gulden gelten (...)*»
Переклад: «…ми з нашим мінцмейстером у Güstrow і любим вірним Mauritz Schacht щодо данської
монети, щоб карбувати нам в імʼя нашого пана, короля Christiern, [на згадку про] його невинне
ув'язнення, у такий спосіб і вигляд домовилися, що він має робити нам гроші, з яких десять
штук становлять один гульден…».

**Kunzel's paraphrase of the standard** (same section): «*Diese Münzen dänischer Währung,
d.h. Vierskillinge, sollten nach folgendem Fuß ausgeprägt werden.: Schrot aus der Marck
(Stück) 63 1/2 · Korn (Lot) 4 1/2 · (Feingehalt o/oo) 281,25 · Rauhmasse (g) 3,683 ·
Feinmasse (g) 1,036*»; «*Als Remedium erhielt Schacht ein Quentchen am Feingehalt, d.h.
15,63 o/oo.*» Переклад: «Ці монети данської валюти, тобто четвертаки, мали карбуватися за
такою стопою: штук з марки 63½ · проба 4½ лота · 281,25 ‰ · сира вага 3,683 г · чиста
1,036 г». «Як ремедіум Schacht отримав один квентхен на пробі, тобто 15,63 ‰». The contract
further grants Schacht for one year two oxen, two pigs, two Drömt of rye and a Last of malt;
after a year the duke would decide on renewal.

**A1 — 2 Feb 1536** (Kunzel): «*(...) weill dir nun bewußt, was itzo dis denisch gelt alhir
inn denmarken helt, So ist unser gnedigs begern du willest mit gemelten valtin ebell, daraus
handeln, und Ime etzliche suma der denischen dobbelten schilling Inn itzigem grats und
schwere, zustellen.*» Переклад: «…оскільки тобі відомо, як тепер тримається ця данська
монета тут, у Данії, то наше милостиве бажання, щоб ти з названим Valtin Ebell про це
домовився і передав йому певну суму данських подвійних шилінгів у теперішній пробі й вазі».
Kunzel's context: the Rostock burgher Valentin Ebell would give «*für 15 seiner dänischen
Doppelschillinge (Vierskillinge) 24 Schilling lübisch*» (перекл.: «за 15 його данських
подвійних шилінгів (четвертаків) 24 шилінги любецькі»); from the Lübeck money new Danish
coin was to be struck and exchanged again «*also fur und fur*» (перекл.: «знову й знову»).

**D — 10 March 1536** (Kunzel): both princes have «*in nhamen (...) khenig Christierns …
etzliche Munze (...) haben munzen und slahen lassen davon Koppenhagen und elbogen [Malmö]
(...) beschwerung getragen*», and promise «*seiner muntze halben (...) nachsehen lassen …
unclagbar machen schadlos halten und widerumb benhemen*». Переклад: «від імені короля
Christiern … велили карбувати певну монету, від якої København і Elbogen [Malmø] зазнали
обтяження»; «щодо своєї монети … подбати, … відшкодувати і знову прийняти її».

## 4. Parameters

### 4.1 Target (V, as Kunzel gives it)

| Coin | Metal | Pieces per mark | Fineness | ‰ | Rough g | Fine g | Tariff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| «Vierskillinge», «dänischer Währung» (A1: «*denischen dobbelten schilling*») | billon | 63½ (mark not named ⚠) | 4½ Lot; remedy 1 Quentchen | 281,25 (remedy 15,63) | 3,683 | 1,036 | 10 = 1 Gulden |

Recomputed: 233,856 g / 63½ = 3,6828 g; × 4,5/16 = 1,0358 g fine; 63½ / 0,28125 = 225,78
pieces per fine mark × 4 ß = 903,1 ß = **56,44 Mark dansk per fine mark**; 10 pieces =
1 Gulden ⇒ 1 Gulden = 40 ß = 2½ Mark dansk, carrying 10,36 g fine silver.

#### 4.1a ⚠ Source-internal discrepancies and gaps

- **Which mark.** Kunzel's gram figures imply 233,856 g (63½ × 3,683 = 233,87 g) — the
  Cologne mark. The quoted clause names no mark; whether the contract says Cologne, Lübeck
  or another mark is not visible in the reading. Jesse (s. 138) treats the Lübeck and
  Cologne marks as the same for silver coin; not verified for this act.
- **Draft or executed act.** Kunzel's note 8 cites «*Brief … 1535 März 28. (Entwurf ?)*»,
  with his own question mark, while his text says Albrecht «*tat öffentlich kund*». The
  register (§4.2) shows that striking began two days later, which fits an agreed contract
  whatever the status of the Schwerin copy.
- **The table is Kunzel's**, not a transcription: the act's own wording of the Schrot, Korn
  and remedy is not quoted.

### 4.2 Outcome (Schütte's register, as Kunzel summarises it)

| Date (1535) | Entry |
|---|---|
| 30 March (Tuesday after Easter) | Peter Kopke delivers silver worth 3 404 fl. and 500 Joachimstaler for recoining |
| 7 April (Wednesday after Quasimodo) | Siegmund von Isfeld 200 Joachimstaler; 2 400 fl. silver through Schütte ⇒ 6 636 fl. 20 ß silver in two months; the same day the first 654 Gulden «dänischer Münze» go to Albrecht at Warnemünde |
| 10 May | 6 753 Gulden «dänisches» Geld to Jacob Bungner and Jacob Moller for Denmark |
| 21 June (Monday after Viti) | 3 568 Gulden to Jacob Moller |
| 2 Aug | 654 Gulden through Heinrich Boldewan, burgomaster of Rostock |
| total | coin worth 11 761 fl. for silver worth 6 636 fl. 20 ß |

Kunzel converts the total, «*bei aller gebotener Vorsicht*», to 117 610 pieces (11 761 × 10)
and «*etwa 433 kg Rauhsilber, bei etwa 122 kg Feinsilber*». Recomputed: 117 610 × 3,6828 g =
433,1 kg; × 1,0358 g = 121,8 kg — **these two weights are the target applied to the count,
not weighed metal**. Face value / silver cost = 11 761 / 6 636,25 = 1,772. «*Hier enden die
Nachrichten über die in Mecklenburg hergestellten dänischen Münzen*» (Kunzel).

### 4.3 Rates in use (A1, A2; Wilcke)

- Reckoning (Wilcke VII s. 238): 1 Mark dansk = 3/5 Mark lybsk; 2½ M d. = 1 Gylden «*i
  Mønt*» = 24 ß lybsk; Grev Christoffer's Firskillingsmønt reckoned at the same value. At
  this reckoning one Güstrow piece (4 ß d.) = 2,4 ß lybsk.
- Rostock, early 1536 (A1): 15 pieces = 24 ß lübisch ⇒ 1,6 ß lübisch the piece — **2/3 of the
  reckoned value** (computed). Albrecht's letter of 17 Feb 1536 (A2) asks his brother Heinrich
  V to press Rostock on the rate.

## 5. Mints and mintmasters

Güstrow (Mecklenburg), mintmaster Moritz Schacht; Joachim Schütte, Vogt of Güstrow, overseer
of the mint from 20 May 1534. In Denmark Albrecht had further mintmasters sent to him by
Jørgen Kock from Malmø (Hans Lutsen and Joachim Schacht, 6 July 1535; three more, 24 Aug
1535) — no standard for their work is known (Kunzel).

## 6. Antecedent and successor instruments; comparison

No Danish act stands behind V. Christian III's side worked by orders to its mintmaster (Junge,
4 Dec 1534); the first legislative act on the war coin is the Reces of 21 Oct 1537
(`1537_reces_moentens_afsaettelse.md`), which halves Christian III's own coin. D (10 March
1536) is the only monetary act of Christian II's side after V and sets no parameter.

| Coin | Standard | Fine g per 4 ß | Mark dansk per fine mark | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frederik I, 4 ß danske 1524 | 14 lod, 78 (or 88) per mark | 2,62 (2,29) | 22,3 (25,1) [computed] | `1524_moentordning_frederik_i.md` §4, §8.5 |
| Christian III, Firskillinger (order 4 Dec 1534) | «*en lødig Mark til fem Lod fint Sølv*», count not given | — | — | Wilcke VII s. 237 |
| Christian III, Firskillinger in Junge's accounts | 4 lod; «*75 $ dansk af Marken fin*» | 0,78 [computed] | 75 | Galster 1934 s. 162 n. 203, s. 191 |
| **Güstrow, V** | **63½, 4½ Lot** | **1,036** | **56,44** [computed] | Kunzel |
| Count Christoffer, 2 Mark (Klipping) 1534–35 | «*5 L. 1 Q. · ca. 16*» | — (2 Mark: c. 4,80 g) | c. 97,5 [computed] | Wilcke VII s. 242 |
| Mecklenburg's own Doppelschilling (Albrecht, 1531) | 60 per mark, 7½ Lot | 1,827 [computed] | — (Lübeck coin) | Jesse 1928 s. 138: «*Seine Doppelschillinge sollen 1531 einen Münzfuß von 60 Stück und 7½ Lot fein haben … Dieser Münzfuß aber entsprach durchaus dem lübischen*» |

Findings from the table:

1. **Not Mecklenburg's standard.** The duchy's own Doppelschilling of the same years is the
   Lübeck coin (60 / 7½ Lot, 1,827 g fine); the Güstrow «Danish» piece carries 57 % of that.
   The act calls it «*dänische Münz*» and fixes it in Danish reckoning (10 = 1 Gulden =
   2½ M d.).
2. **No identical Danish grid is known.** The contract sits between Frederik I's 4 ß of 1524
   (2½× the silver) and Christian III's later war Firskillinger (75 M d. per fine mark); in
   fineness it is close to Junge's 4–5 lod order of December 1534.
3. **One campaign, by the sources.** One contract (for one year, renewal open), one run March
   – August 1535, «*Hier enden die Nachrichten*». Albrecht's plan of February 1536 to recoin
   Lübeck money into Danish coin «*fur und fur*» is a plan; whether anything was struck under
   it is not known from the sources.

**Coins whose weight fits the contract figure — hypothesis, not established (§0b).** The
weights in our data of the other Count's-Feud 4 ß against the target 3,683 g:

| Coin | Specimen | Weight | Δ to 3,683 g |
|---|---|---|---|
| Galster 91, 4 Skilling 1535 (danskmoent: København) | Bruun Part III, lot 11161, p. 68 (`dk-bruun-4149`) | 3,56 g | −3,3 % |
| Galster 91 | KMM 156730 | 3,69 g | +0,2 % |
| Galster 91 | danskmoent (Galster page, «ca.») | ca. 4,1 g | +11,3 % |
| Galster 86, 4 Skilling 1535 (Malmø) | — | no weight in our data | — |
| Galster 85 itself (Güstrow) | danskmoent («ca.») | ca. 3,35 g | −9,0 % |

The two weighed Galster 91 specimens lie at the Güstrow target, so the Malmø / København
four-skilling pieces may have been struck on the same formula. *Fra Denar til Daler* s. 480
names the two Malmø kinds of 4 ß (Christian II's title; Christian II's and Christoffer's) as
the commonest coins of the party. What would settle it: an assay of Galster 86 or 91, or a
mint record of the Count's mints in Malmø / København; until then the weight agreement is
only that. Kunzel's own reading is that the Güstrow coin served as the pattern for the
Copenhagen Beutepfennige («*als Muster für die Beutepfennige*») — a statement about the type,
not the standard.

## 8. Open questions

1. **Which mark the contract names** (§4.1a) — settled by the text of Acta monetaria XVI.
2. **Draft or executed** («*Entwurf ?*») — the same file; production two days later points to
   an agreed contract.
3. **Galster 86 / 91 on the Güstrow formula?** (§6) — an assay of either, or a Malmø /
   København mint account.
4. **Galster 85 «Malmø» in KMM 150191 / 150192** (§7) — the KMM object pages (images,
   inventory notes) or the Malmø-type die study; meanwhile both readings stay.
5. **Kurant or Scheide** for a piece that is the main coin of a debased Danish currency —
   a curator decision when Lybsk Møntfod period III is reviewed.
6. **An «unknown 8 skilling»** (*Fra Denar til Daler* s. 480, «*måske*») — no source names
   one; nothing to record unless a specimen or an account appears.
